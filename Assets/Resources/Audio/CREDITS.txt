  Source: "072807_Heavy Machine Gun (.50 Caliber)" by freesound_community, from
    Pixabay - https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-072807-heavy-machine-gun-50-caliber-39765/
  License: Pixabay Content License - https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
    Free for commercial use, no attribution required. You may NOT resell or
    redistribute the sound files themselves as-is (e.g. as a stock-audio pack).
    Baked into the game as SFX = an allowed use.
  Processing (ffmpeg): trimmed lead silence, 4 variants at slightly different
    pitch (1.00 / 0.97 / 0.94 / 0.985) and tail length (1.1-1.45s), peak
    normalised. No EQ/drive - the source already has the deep .50-cal weight.

    https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-066496-heavy-machine-gun-50-caliber-28243/

BulletImpact/impact_1..3.ogg  -  enemy bullet hitting the player's car (Projectile.cs)
  Source: "BLLTImpt - Samsung Galaxy Smartphone, CU_On Metal" by Nicholas Judy /
    designerschoice, from Pixabay
    https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-blltimpt-samsung-galaxy-smartphone-cu-on-metal-nicholas-judy-tdc-488782/
  License: Pixabay Content License (same terms as the rest).
  ffmpeg: 3 near-identical hits sliced from the 43s take, mono, pitched to ~0.90
  for car-panel weight, low-mid bump, highs rolled off (lowpass ~4.6k + high-
  shelf cut) and attack eased so it doesn't grab the ear, ~0.22s, normalised to
  a gentle -4 dBFS. Projectile round-robins them, +/-6% pitch jitter, 2D, only
  when the bullet damages the "Player"-tagged car. Playback level is the player
  setting GameSettings.BulletHitVolume (raw 0..0.4, default 0.23, 0 = off).
  The settings menu will show this as GameSettings.BulletHitVolumePercent, a
  0..100 slider (100 = 0.4); 0.23 default reads as ~57.5.

Explosion/explosion.ogg  -  elimination explosion (DamageFx.cs)
  Source: "Explosion FX" by SoundReality, from Pixabay (48 kHz source)
    https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-explosion-fx-343683/
  License: Pixabay Content License (same terms as the gun above).
  ffmpeg: mono 44.1 kHz, trimmed lead silence, 24 Hz high-pass, +2.3 dB (~+30%,
  user's call), trimmed to ~3.6s, smooth exponential fade-out on the tail,
  limiter to catch the boosted peak.
  (Superseded: Kenney sci-fi "explosionCrunch"; Pixabay "Loud Explosion",
  "Explosion_01", "Medium Explosion".)
